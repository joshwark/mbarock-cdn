What MBA Rock is

MBA Rock is a complete business education delivered as a musical album. Ten modules covering the same surface area as a top-tier core MBA — strategy, cash flow, moats, unit economics, operations, capital, marketing, analytics, innovation, leadership. Seventy-four lessons. Forty-nine original songs that anchor each module's framework in long-term memory. Eighteen founder calculators you can run on your real numbers. A 10-section Capstone you graduate with — not a hat, but a written, defensible business plan.

Why it exists

The MBA system asks for two years and roughly $200,000. The operators I respect — founders, indie builders, people running real companies in the cracks of busy lives — can't and shouldn't pay that cost. Most of them have already chosen the building path. They want the reasoning behind an MBA, not the credential.

So I built it for them. Self-paced. Operator-first. Honest about what cash flow really does, what brand really is, what moats really cost. Calculators that actually open on a Tuesday. Songs that make the frameworks portable across days.

Who built it

I'm Joshua Wark — entrepreneur and creative director running a small portfolio of brands in food, services, education, and entertainment. I dropped out of the academic-credential path early and learned business by building. MBA Rock is the curriculum I wrote backward from twenty years of operating mistakes.

I built it with help from Claude, an AI agent made by Anthropic. Claude served as chief of staff across the whole build — execution, code, content drafting, accessibility audits, curriculum mapping against the Wharton/Stanford/HBS core, end-to-end QA. I made the calls; Claude shipped the work. It's the cleanest division of labor I've ever had with a co-builder, and it's exactly the operating model I want to teach.

About the AI partnership: Honesty is a feature, not a footnote. Every framework, calculator, song lyric, and lesson on this site was written under human direction with AI execution support. The accessibility work, the curriculum coverage, and the depth of every lesson were reviewed by both of us before shipping.

What you get

  • Every lesson includes a video, the song for the module, four to seven core concepts, a "take action" prompt that pushes directly into your Capstone, and a quiz.
  • The Operator's Console hosts all 18 calculators in one workspace — cash flow, runway, CAC/LTV, unit economics, pricing, hiring, and more.
  • The Capstone walks you through 10 sections, one per module, building toward a complete written plan for whatever you're actually building.
  • Certificates for each module plus a final Operator's Certificate, all with LinkedIn add-to-profile deep links.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility across every page. Keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, captions and transcripts on every song and video.

Who it's for

  • Founders building right now who want the MBA reasoning without the two-year detour.
  • Operators inside companies aiming for their next role.
  • Creatives and freelancers turning craft into a business.
  • Anyone who got into b-school and decided to build instead.
  • Institutions — accelerators, b-schools, corporate L&D programs — looking for a pre-built operator-focused curriculum to license. See the institutional page for partnership terms.

Who it isn't for

  • People shopping for the credential alone. MBA Rock is not an accredited degree program. You'll get certificates and a portfolio piece (the Capstone), not a Master's.
  • Anyone looking for a passive course library. MBA Rock has homework. The take-action prompts and the Capstone require real work on your real numbers.
