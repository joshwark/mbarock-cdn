Accessibility Statement

MBA Rock is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards.

WCAG 2.1 Level AA — Target Accessibility widget active

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. They define three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

MBA Rock targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance. This statement describes how MBA Rock conforms, identifies any non-conformant areas, and explains how to report issues.

Measures taken to support accessibility

MBA Rock takes the following measures to ensure accessibility:

  • Includes accessibility as part of our mission statement and core values.
  • Includes accessibility throughout our internal policies.
  • Designs with accessibility as a first-class requirement, not a post-hoc adjustment.
  • Provides an on-site accessibility widget that lets users adjust the experience to their needs (text size, font, contrast, cursor size, animation, reading aids).
  • Tests against WCAG 2.1 Level AA criteria on every public page release.
  • Welcomes user feedback and responds within five business days.

What our accessibility widget does

Look for the floating accessibility button (the ♿ icon in the corner of every page). It opens a panel where you can adjust:

FeatureOptions

Your preferences are saved in your browser and persist across visits. You can reset everything to default at any time from the widget panel.

Compatibility with browsers and assistive technology

MBA Rock is designed to be compatible with the following:

  • Latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
  • VoiceOver (macOS, iOS).
  • NVDA and JAWS (Windows).
  • TalkBack (Android).
  • iOS and Android system-level accessibility settings (Dynamic Type, Smart Invert, Display Zoom).

If you use a different browser or assistive technology and encounter an issue, please let us know — we'd like to support you.

Known limitations

Despite our best efforts, the following limitations may exist:

  • Embedded third-party content: Some embedded video players (where used) may not be fully keyboard accessible. We are working with vendors to address this.
  • Member-generated content: Notes and Capstone responses that members create are not subject to our accessibility review.
  • Audio descriptions: Currently in progress. Full transcripts are available for all video content.

How to report accessibility issues

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of MBA Rock. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please contact us:

  • Email: help@bettertogetherbranding.ai
  • Response time: We try to respond within five business days.

When reporting an issue, please include:

  1. The URL of the page where the issue occurred.
  2. A description of what happened and what you expected.
  3. Your browser, operating system, and assistive technology (if any).

Formal complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to file a formal complaint with the appropriate enforcement body. For US residents, the ADA.gov website offers guidance on enforcement and complaint procedures.
