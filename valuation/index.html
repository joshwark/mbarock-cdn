Where MBA Rock sits

More built than typical pre-seed (which usually has only a deck + prototype). Less revenue than typical seed (which usually has early MRR or active paying users). Closest analog: "seed-stage edtech with fully-built product, pre-monetization" — historically $5M–$15M.

  • Outschool — $45M Series A at ~$320M (2020); marketplace + recurring revenue. Significantly more mature.
  • Maven — $25M Series A at ~$100M post (2021); cohort-based courses, pre-significant revenue.
  • Section — ~$50M post (2022 round); online business ed for professionals. Closest target-market analog.
  • Hone — ~$45M post (2021); cohort-based corporate training. Closest to institutional licensing model.
Method 2 output: $5M – $15M, centered on the typical post-money for fully-built, pre-revenue B2B+B2C edtech with strong institutional pipeline.
