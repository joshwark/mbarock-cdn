FAQ

Questions worth answering before you click buy.

If something isn’t covered here, write us — we’ll answer in plain language.

Is this really a full MBA curriculum, or just a marketing line?

The curriculum maps to the same 10 functional areas a top-25 MBA covers: finance, strategy, leadership, unit economics, operations, capital, marketing, execution, analytics, and innovation. The format is different — songs, lessons, calculators, capstone — but the frameworks are the same. We use Porter, Kim & Mauborgne, Kotter, Christensen, the usual canon — plus the operating playbooks people actually run.

How long does it take to finish?

Most people complete the program in 60 days at a sustainable pace (about 5–7 hours/week). You can go faster — there’s no schedule — and you keep lifetime access, so it’s fine if life gets in the way and you come back in three months.

What’s the Operator’s Certificate worth?

It proves you completed every comprehension check and shipped a full Capstone. LinkedIn renders it in your Licenses & Certifications section the same way it renders any pro cert. We don’t claim it’s equivalent to a degree — we claim it’s evidence you can actually build the thing the degree teaches you about.

Why songs?

Retention. Concepts set to music stick. You can listen on a run, on a plane, while you’re cooking — the frameworks rehearse themselves. The lesson page on the site is where you go deeper: read the concept, run the calculator, fill in the Capstone field.

Refund policy?

30 days, no questions. If MBA Rock isn’t the right format for how you learn, write us within 30 days of purchase and we’ll refund in full. For the monthly plan, cancel anytime and keep what you’ve earned to date.

Who is this not for?

If you want a credential primarily for resume optics and have no plans to actually run or build a business, an accredited MBA is probably a better fit. MBA Rock is for people doing the work. We optimize for what you’ll ship, not what you can list.
