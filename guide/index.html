Quick Start

Five steps. About 15 minutes.

If you do nothing else today, do these five things in order. Everything else builds from here.

1

Open your Dashboard

It's your home base. Shows your current streak, the next lesson to play, your Capstone progress, and a link to every other tool.

→ www.mbarock.com/dashboard
2

Play Lesson 1 of Module 1

"Oxygen: Cash Flow Management." Hit the audio overview button, follow along with the lesson page, then play the song. Total time: 8 minutes.

The lessons are album-paced — short enough to do during your morning coffee.
3

Take the comprehension quiz

5 questions at the end of each lesson. Pass it (60%+) to mark the lesson complete and unlock the next one. You can retake immediately if you miss.

Quizzes are how you earn the 10 Module Marks + Operator's Certificate.
4

Do the "Take Action"

Every lesson ships with one practical action — usually a small calculation or a paragraph to draft about your own business. Type your answer into the lesson's Take Action box. It saves automatically and fills the matching Capstone section.

This is how the Capstone "builds itself" as you go.
5

Open the Capstone tab to watch it fill in

By the end of the course, this 10-section document is your operating business plan — investor-grade. Each lesson's Take Action lands in the matching section automatically.

→ www.mbarock.com/capstone

That's it. Repeat for the next lesson tomorrow. You'll finish in about 90 days at one lesson/day, faster if you binge a module on the weekend.

FAQ

Things people ask in the first 48 hours.

How long does the whole course take?

About 90 days at one lesson per day. Some lessons are 5 minutes, some are 20. Most members finish a module in a week. If you binge on the weekend, you can compress the whole thing into 6 weeks. Lifetime access — no rush.

Do I have to do the modules in order?

You can jump around. The 10-module spine is designed in order (Cash → Markets → Operations → Growth, etc.) and earlier modules unlock concepts that later modules build on — but if you have a specific business need today, jump to it. Use the Founder Diagnostic (coming soon) to see which modules to prioritize given your stage.

What if I don't pass a quiz?

Retake it. No limit, no penalty. Quizzes are checkpoints, not gates. You can also revisit the lesson and audio at any time.

Do my Capstone notes save automatically?

Yes — every keystroke. There's no Save button. If you ever see the "Saved ✓" indicator flicker, that's the auto-save firing. Your work persists across devices when you're signed in.

Can I share my Capstone / Dossier with a non-member?

Yes. The Dossier export is a portable PDF — share anywhere. The Capstone has a "share link" feature in the works (Q3 2026); for now, export to PDF and send.

Can I add my Module Marks to LinkedIn?

Yes. Each Mark has a "Add to LinkedIn" deeplink that pre-fills LinkedIn's "Add a license or certification" form. One click, done. The Operator's Certificate works the same way.

I bought MBA Rock — where does my $499 go?

Curriculum building (audio production, the 49 songs, lesson writing, the Operator's Console, the Capstone builder), hosting, customer support, and continuous improvement. You pay once. You keep lifetime access. Every future lesson, calculator, or feature is included.

Something's broken / not loading.

Sorry. Two things to try first: (1) hard-refresh the page (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows), (2) check that you're signed in to your MBA Rock account. If it's still broken, email help@bettertogetherbranding.ai with the URL and a screenshot — we'll fix it fast.

Can my team use my login?

Your account is for you. For team access (20+ seats) we have institutional pricing starting at $349/seat/year. Email help@bettertogetherbranding.ai for a walk-through.
