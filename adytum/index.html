How it works

The pipeline, end-to-end.

Linear. Predictable. No fluff.

1

Finish the course

Complete at least the Operator's Capstone (10 sections) and earn the Operator's Certificate. We don't review submissions from incomplete members — the work is the qualification.

2

Export your Dossier

One click in your dashboard. The Dossier is the investor-grade PDF the program is built around — your plan, metrics, and positioning compressed into one readable artifact.

3

Submit through your member account

From your dashboard, hit "Submit to Adytum Pipeline." Choose: Investment Desk, Brand Studio, or both. Optional notes (15 lines max).

4

Initial review — 7 to 14 days

We read every submission. You'll get one of three responses: Pass (with the reason), Bring more (with specific asks), or Move forward (scoping call scheduled).

5

Scoping conversation

If you move forward: a structured 45-minute conversation. Investment Desk reviews terms and check size; Brand Studio reviews scope and member rate. No commitment yet — both sides decide after.

6

Engagement

Investment: term sheet or formal pre-seed intro. Brand Studio: signed creative brief and a calendar. Both paths assume you remain the operator — we back you and amplify, we don't take over.

What we look for

What clears the bar.

Plain English. No mystery. You can self-assess before submitting.

  • ✓   Clarity of plan — your Capstone reads like an operator wrote it, not a consultant.
  • ✓   Unit economics that work — CAC, LTV, payback period, margins; numbers, not metaphors.
  • ✓   Operator skin in the game — even pre-launch is fine; what matters is your move history.
  • ✓   A real reason to back you — for Investment, a path to capital efficiency; for Brand, a category you can own.
  • ✓   An honest narrative — no inflation, no fairy-tale TAM. We'd rather you say what's hard than what's perfect.
FAQ

The honest questions.

Is this guaranteed?

No. The pipeline is a private channel, not a promise of capital or services. Most submissions don't move past the initial review — that's the point. Members of MBA Rock get access to the channel; everyone still earns the engagement.

Why don't you offer this separately?

Because the Capstone + Dossier are how we evaluate fit. The program produces the artifact we need to review you. Without that, our review process is the same un-scaleable one as everyone else's. The course replaces the first three meetings.

How much capital are you writing?

Adytum's friends-and-family checks have historically been $25k–$250k. Pre-seed introductions can be larger. The Adytum desk underwrites case-by-case; we don't publish a fund vintage.

What does the Brand Studio actually do?

The same brand stack we use on our own portfolio: positioning, identity, naming, voice, brand book, launch assets, web. Member rate is roughly half of our standard agency rate. Engagements typically run 6–10 weeks.

Can I submit more than once?

Yes — but wait 90 days between submissions and bring something materially new (new data, new traction, new positioning). Repeated submissions without change are auto-passed.

What if I'm not a member yet?

The pipeline opens the moment you complete the Operator's Certificate inside MBA Rock. Join here — Founders' rate is $499 through June 30, 2026.

Is there a deadline to submit?

No. The pipeline is always open to active members. We process submissions monthly.

Who reviews the submissions?

Joshua Wark (founder, MBA Rock + Adytum + Adytum) does the first read. For Investment Desk submissions that move forward, our broader investment committee weighs in. For Brand Studio engagements, our creative director scopes the work.
