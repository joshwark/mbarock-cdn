Why a podcast version

You can read about Porter's five forces. You can watch a video about cohort retention. But neither of those will be playing in your head when you're staring at your pricing page on a Tuesday afternoon. A song will.

The full MBA Rock course is at mbarock.com — that's where the explainer videos, calculators, capstone, and certificate live. This feed is just the album: the memory-hook layer, free for anyone with a podcast app.
