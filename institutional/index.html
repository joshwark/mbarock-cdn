FAQ

What teams ask us first.

How does the admin dashboard work?

Each organization gets a private admin view showing every seat-holder, their lesson completion, quiz pass rate, Capstone section completion, and certificate count. Export CSV anytime. Filter by cohort, department, or custom segment. Available at all tiers.

Can we run a pilot before committing to a full cohort?

Yes. We'll set up a 10-seat, 30-day pilot at the Team rate ($349/seat) so you can see retention, completion, and feedback from your own people before signing for a year. Pilot fees apply to your annual contract if you proceed.

What does "annual license" mean exactly?

Each seat is licensed for 12 months from activation. You can swap a learner out (e.g. someone leaves) anytime — the seat carries on to whoever you reassign it to. Renewals are auto-priced at the same per-seat rate unless your seat count crosses into a new tier.

Do learners keep access if our contract ends?

Seat access ends with the contract. Learners keep their Capstone plan (downloadable PDF), their Dossier (downloadable PDF), and their certificate records (verifiable forever via the cert ID). They can convert to an individual MBA Rock lifetime license at a 30% loyalty rate.

Can we co-brand the experience?

Yes, at the Cohort and Enterprise tiers. We add your logo, your program name, and optionally your color accent to the learner-facing surfaces. White-label (your domain, your full brand) is Enterprise-only.

What about LMS integration?

At Enterprise, we support SCORM and xAPI export so your existing LMS (Cornerstone, Workday Learning, Docebo, Canvas, Moodle, etc.) can ingest progress and completion events. SSO is available at Cohort and above (Google Workspace native; Okta and Azure AD via setup).

Is the curriculum customizable?

The core 10 modules are the core 10 modules — they're built around the universal operator playbook. At Enterprise, we'll commission a custom module for your sector (e.g., a Music Label Operations module, a Restaurant Group P&L module, a Pharma Commercial Ops module) at fixed-fee development cost. Custom courses for partner institutions typically ship in 7–14 days, not weeks. Custom modules for sector-specific Enterprise needs (Music Label Operations, Restaurant Group P&L, Pharma Commercial Ops, etc.) ship in 14–21 days.

How do certificates work for organizations?

Each learner earns up to 11 certificates (10 Module Marks + 1 Operator's Certificate). Each carries a verification URL (e.g. mbarock.com/verify/CERT-ID) and a LinkedIn "Add to Profile" deep link. Your admin can export a roster of who's earned what at any point.

How fast can we get started?

From "yes" to "first learner logged in" is typically 5–7 business days for Team and Cohort tiers, longer for Enterprise (SSO setup, white-label provisioning, custom modules). Book the walk-through — we can scope the timeline in one call.
