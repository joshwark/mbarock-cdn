The honest take

Each of these works for a different kind of person. Pick the one that matches what you're actually trying to do — not what sounds prestigious.

Pick MBA Rock if…

You're building a business right now.

You need a plan you can hand to a co-founder, an investor, or your own future self. You learn by applying. You want lifetime access without a $200K bill. You want to start today.

Pick a Traditional MBA if…

You need the credential, not the knowledge.

You're a consultant, banker, or career-switcher where "MBA from Wharton" opens doors that nothing else does. You also want the alumni network for life — that's the asset, and it's real.

Pick Coursera if…

You want to add a credential to a résumé.

You're a job seeker, you have time, and you want a stackable certificate from a known university. You're not building a business; you're filling a gap.

Pick a Maven cohort if…

You learn best live, with a group, in 6 weeks.

You want the structure of a specific instructor's sprint, you don't mind a live schedule, and you'll do most of your learning in the discussion. Strong for a narrow topic; expensive for full-breadth.
