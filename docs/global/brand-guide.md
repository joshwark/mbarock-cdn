# MBA Rock — Brand Guide v1.0

**Status:** Living document. v1.0 frozen 2026-05-12. Updates require a version bump and changelog entry.

This is the canonical document for every visual, verbal, and structural decision in the MBA Rock brand. If something contradicts what's here, this wins.

---

## 1 · Identity

### 1.1 The names

| Use | Format |
|---|---|
| Brand name (short) | **MBA Rock** |
| Long form | **Musical Business Album Rock** |
| What MBA stands for | **Musical Business Album** *(never "Music Business Album")* |
| Tagline | **Listen. Learn. Live.** |
| One-line description | *"Every framework an MBA teaches, condensed into songs and short lessons — so you can build a real business from it."* |

### 1.2 Mission

MBA Rock exists to make operator-grade business education accessible, memorable, and applied — by converting it into songs you replay and a Capstone you build alongside the course.

### 1.3 Audience

Founders. Operators. Ambitious professionals who want to build, not credential. People who learn better by ear and by doing than by reading textbooks. Adults age 25–55, comfortable with the internet, allergic to slow.

### 1.4 The promise

*Finish the course and walk out with a real business — operating system, pitch, 90-day plan.*

The course is the path. The Capstone is the artifact. The mission is the work.

## 2 · Voice & Tone

### 2.1 Voice rules

```
- Confident, plainspoken, never preachy.
- Hooks come from music. Concepts come from business.
- Short sentences over long ones. Verbs over adjectives.
- Refer to the course as "MBA Rock" — never "the program" or "the platform."
- The reader is an operator, not a student. Speak to them like a peer.
- Cite frameworks (Porter, Christensen, Kim) without name-dropping for effect.
- Don't pretend it's easy. The course is the easy part — the work is the work.
```

### 2.2 Don't list

```
- "Master your X" / "Unlock your X" — generic guru-speak.
- "Game-changing" / "Revolutionary" / "Disruptive" — adjectives without evidence.
- "Just imagine if…" — manipulative hypotheticals.
- "MBA = Music Business Album" — IT'S MUSICAL.
- Long sentences when short ones land. Dependent clauses are usually decoration.
- "We at MBA Rock" — first-person plural in marketing copy reads insecure.
- Emojis in body copy. Acceptable in chips/buttons. Forbidden in headlines.
```

### 2.3 Examples

| Off-brand | On-brand |
|---|---|
| "Unlock the secrets of successful businesses through our revolutionary curriculum." | "Every framework an MBA takes two years to teach. Condensed. Sung. Applied." |
| "Are you ready to take your business to the next level?" | "Build the business. The course is the path." |
| "Our world-class instructors will guide you on a transformative journey." | "Fifty lessons. Two hundred concepts. One operating system, built section by section." |

### 2.4 Sentence patterns we use

- **Naming the thing**: "Cash is the fact. Profit is the opinion."
- **Setting the bar**: "If you can't say it in one sentence, you haven't locked it yet."
- **Closing with a move**: "Knowing this number every Friday changes how you spend Monday."

These cadences are signatures. Use them.

## 3 · Logo System

### 3.1 The marks

| Mark | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Primary** — headphones + "MBA Rock" + "LISTEN. LEARN. LIVE." | Lesson cert outro, course dashboard hero, splash screens. The "tagline visible" version. |
| **Secondary** — headphones + "MBA Rock" + "VOL. 1" | Cohort-specific surfaces. Marketing materials tied to volume releases. |
| **Mark only** — headphones glyph alone | Favicon, app icon, small spaces (under 80px), social avatars. |
| **Wordmark only** — "MBA Rock" in custom letterforms | Single-line lockups, footer, corner branding. |
| **Logo video** — 10-second animated mark | Lesson completion moment, welcome splash, intro to videos. |

### 3.2 Clear space

Around all logo usage, maintain clear space equal to the height of the "M" in MBA. Nothing — text, image, edge — encroaches.

### 3.3 Minimum sizes

| Surface | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Print | 0.5 inch tall |
| Digital | 32px tall (mark only) · 48px (mark + wordmark) · 80px (full primary) |
| Favicon | 16×16 px (use simplified mark variant) |

### 3.4 Color variations

- **Default**: white headphones + orange "MBA ROCK" + white tagline on black background
- **Inverted (light surfaces)**: black headphones + black "MBA ROCK" on white/cream — kept as a fallback only
- **Monochrome (printed b&w)**: solid black on white

The orange must stay orange. Never recolor the headphones to anything other than white or black.

### 3.5 Don't

❌ Don't squish, stretch, or rotate the logo
❌ Don't add drop-shadows, glows, or 3D effects
❌ Don't recolor the "MBA ROCK" text outside the palette
❌ Don't place on visually busy backgrounds without clear space
❌ Don't pair with other logos within the clear-space zone

## 4 · Color System

### 4.1 Primary palette

| Token | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `navy` | `#0B1F3A` | 11, 31, 58 | Headlines, structural elements, primary CTA on cream |
| `ink` | `#0E1116` | 14, 17, 22 | Body text, deep surfaces |
| `orange` | `#F26B1F` | 242, 107, 31 | THE accent. Logo, CTAs, numerals, kickers, brand mark |
| `cream` | `#FAF8F5` | 250, 248, 245 | Page surfaces, large background fills |
| `paper` | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Card backgrounds |

### 4.2 Extended palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `orange-soft` | `#FBE2D0` | Selection backgrounds, hover tints |
| `rule` | `#E5E0D6` | Borders, dividers, neutral lines |
| `muted` | `#8A8A8A` | Secondary text, captions, metadata |
| `success` | `#1D8444` | Saved states, completion, positive metrics |
| `warning` | `#D49F1C` | In-progress, attention-needed |
| `danger` | `#A01A1A` | Errors, negative metrics, destructive actions |

### 4.3 Accessibility

| Combination | Contrast | WCAG AA |
|---|---|---|
| `navy` on `cream` | 14.9:1 | ✓ AAA |
| `ink` on `cream` | 17.1:1 | ✓ AAA |
| `orange` on `cream` | 4.1:1 | ✓ AA large text only |
| `orange` on `ink` | 4.2:1 | ✓ AA large text only |
| white on `orange` | 3.5:1 | ✗ — use only for non-text decoration |
| `navy` on `paper` | 17.5:1 | ✓ AAA |

**Rule:** Orange is for headlines, kickers, and decorative use. Body copy is always navy or ink on cream/paper.

### 4.4 Don't

❌ Don't introduce new colors without versioning this guide
❌ Don't use orange for body text — it fails contrast at small sizes
❌ Don't pair navy and ink directly (too close to read distinctly)
❌ Don't use gradient fills outside the specified hero gradient

### 4.5 Module color spine

Each of the 10 modules carries a canonical color. Every Capstone section (which is 1:1 with a module) inherits that color. Lesson page kickers and section numerals shift to the module color via a CSS custom property `--mr5-accent` set per page.

| # | Module | Color | Hex | Soft tint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Finance Fundamentals | Navy | `#0B2545` | `#E6EAF1` |
| M2 | Strategy & Competitive Advantage | Royal blue | `#1A3A6B` | `#E5EBF2` |
| M3 | People & Leadership | Burgundy | `#8B1A1A` | `#F4E4E4` |
| M4 | Unit Economics & Growth Metrics | Forest green | `#1A5C2A` | `#E2EEE5` |
| M5 | Operations & Technology | Royal purple | `#4A1A6B` | `#ECE3F2` |
| M6 | Growth & Capital | Sienna | `#6B3A0B` | `#F1E8DE` |
| M7 | Marketing & Brand | Brand orange | `#F26B1F` | `#FCE5D6` |
| M8 | Operations & Execution | Slate | `#475569` | `#E6EAEE` |
| M9 | Analytics & Decision Making | Indigo | `#3B4F8C` | `#E5E9F1` |
| M10 | Innovation & Future (Capstone) | Gold | `#A88735` | `#F4ECDB` |

**Rules:**
- Module color = identity color for that module. Use it on the kicker, section numerals, the §-pill, and the section's left-edge accent strip in the Capstone.
- Soft tint = background-only. Field hover, pill backgrounds, low-contrast surfaces.
- Brand orange `#F26B1F` is still the global accent for cross-module surfaces (homepage CTAs, sales page, Operator's Console, Arcade). Module colors only override inside a module's own context.
- Read color from data: `lessons.v2.json:modules[].color` is the source. Never hardcode hex per module in product code — read the data file.

### 4.6 The spine visual

The Capstone page header carries a 10-segment color spine — one colored bar per section. Filled segments brighten as the user completes sections. Use the same spine pattern in:
- Capstone page (header strip)
- Course dashboard (progress visualization)
- Module landing pages (the active module's color is filled, others dimmed)
- Certificate visual (module mark uses its module color; Operator's Cert uses navy with all 10 spine colors as a footer ribbon)

## 5 · Typography

### 5.1 The pair

| Token | Family | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `serif` | **Fraunces** (Google Fonts, weights 400–700) | Headlines, lesson titles, concept titles, deep dives, calculator results, lyrics |
| `sans` | **Inter** (Google Fonts, weights 400–700) | Body text, UI labels, navigation, buttons, metadata |
| `mono` | **ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo** | Code, URLs, IDs, system labels |

Google Fonts CDN load (single line):
```
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap
```

### 5.2 Type scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `display` | 54px / 3.375rem | 600 | 1.05 | Hero headlines (lesson page H1) |
| `h1` | 42px / 2.625rem | 600 | 1.1 | Section headlines (Capstone, Console) |
| `h2` | 32px / 2rem | 600 | 1.15 | Sub-sections |
| `h3` | 24px / 1.5rem | 600 | 1.2 | Card headlines |
| `subtitle` | 21px / 1.3125rem | 400 italic | 1.45 | Italic subtitles under headlines |
| `body-large` | 18px / 1.125rem | 400 | 1.7 | Deep dive body, lyrics, longform |
| `body` | 15.5px / 0.97rem | 400 | 1.6 | Default UI body |
| `small` | 13.5px / 0.84rem | 500 | 1.55 | Metadata, captions |
| `eyebrow` | 11px / 0.69rem | 700 letter-spaced 0.18em uppercase | 1 | Kickers, section labels |
| `numeral` | (varies) | 600 Fraunces | 1 | Result numbers, big stats, counters |

### 5.3 Weights used

- **400** — body copy (both serif and sans)
- **500** — emphasized body, sub-titles, italic accents
- **600** — headlines, numerals, button labels
- **700** — eyebrows, all-caps labels, status chips

No 300 (too light for premium feel). No 800/900 (too aggressive).

### 5.4 Italic usage

Fraunces in italic is a signature voice device — used for:
- Subtitles under headlines (5–25 words)
- Inline editorial quotes
- Interpretation/voice-over text in calculators
- Closing one-liner under a section

Inter italic is rare — used only for genuine quotes, foreign words, or scientific names.

### 5.5 Don't

❌ Don't mix more than two type families (Fraunces + Inter — full stop)
❌ Don't use Fraunces for body text under 16px
❌ Don't use ALL CAPS outside the `eyebrow` style
❌ Don't add letter-spacing to body text
❌ Don't underline anything except links

## 6 · Iconography

### 6.1 System rules

- **Monoline** — single stroke weight (1.6–1.8px on a 24×24 grid)
- **24×24 px viewBox** — all icons fit
- **Rounded line caps + joins** — soft, organic, matches Fraunces's serif curves
- **`currentColor`** — icons inherit the surrounding text color
- **No fills** — outline only (exception: filled "complete" state)
- **No multi-color** — single color from the palette, set by `currentColor`

## 7 · Layout & Spacing

### 7.1 Spacing scale

Multiples of 4px. Use ONLY these values:

```
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48 · 64 · 80 · 96
```

### 7.2 Container widths

| Surface | Max width |
|---|---|
| Lesson page body | 1080px |
| Reading/long-form (deep dive) | 780px |
| Capstone | 1080px |
| Console | 1280px (sidebar + content) |
| Modal/dialog | 680px |
| Mobile (all) | 100% with 18–24px gutter |

### 7.3 Border radius

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `radius-sm` | 4px | Inputs, small chips |
| `radius` | 8px | Buttons, cards inside cards |
| `radius-lg` | 14px | Primary cards, modals |
| `radius-xl` | 28px | Hero blocks, certificates |
| `pill` | 999px | Status chips, floating actions |

### 7.4 Shadows

Use sparingly. Two levels:

```css
--shadow-sm: 0 1px 3px rgba(11,31,58,0.04);
--shadow-md: 0 12px 28px -10px rgba(11,31,58,0.12);
```

No `box-shadow` deeper than `--shadow-md`. No multiple shadows stacked.

### 7.5 Breakpoints

| Token | Width | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `sm` | < 680px | Mobile / phone |
| `md` | 680–1024px | Tablet / narrow desktop |
| `lg` | 1025px+ | Desktop / wide |

Single column under 680px, two-column above. The Console uses a fixed 300px sidebar above 900px and stacks below.

## 8 · Imagery

### 8.1 Photography style

- **Tone**: warm, grounded, never glossy
- **Lighting**: natural, golden hour or soft overcast — never flash
- **Subject**: founders at work, real workspaces, hands-on moments. Not stock-photo handshakes.
- **Avoid**: corporate clichés (suited people in conference rooms), aspirational lifestyle (private jets, mansions)

### 8.2 Illustration (when used)

- **Style**: editorial line drawings, single-color (orange or navy)
- **Subject matter**: business concepts, frameworks, music metaphors
- **Avoid**: 3D renders, gradients, anything that looks like a 2018 SaaS startup illustration kit

### 8.3 Video

The animated logo video sets the standard: cinematic but quick, audio is a feature (not muted-by-default), 10 seconds maximum for any brand surface.

Lesson videos are different — those are utilitarian, talking-head style, follow the lesson script portfolio.

## 9 · Voice in motion

### 9.1 The eyebrow

```
M1 · FINANCE FUNDAMENTALS
```

Module ID + ' · ' + module title in 11px Inter 700 letter-spaced 0.18em uppercase orange. Sits above every section headline.

### 9.2 The numeral

Fraunces 600 at display size for any big metric. Tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so digits align in columns.

Examples:
- "6.7 months" (Cash Runway result)
- "01 02 03 04" (lesson numbering)
- "$2.4M" (NPV)

### 9.3 The italic close

Sections close with an italic-serif one-liner that names the move:

> *"Knowing this number every Friday changes how you spend Monday."*

> *"The price holds because the work is real — and the work is yours forever."*

> *"Strategy is the choices you make, not the aspirations you write."*

This is a signature voice device. Use it sparingly (one per section, not every paragraph) and only when the line earns its keep.

## 11 · Don't (the consolidated list)

❌ Don't write "MBA = Music Business Album" — it's **Musical**
❌ Don't use emojis in headlines or body
❌ Don't use orange for body text
❌ Don't add a third font family
❌ Don't restyle the logo
❌ Don't use shadows deeper than `--shadow-md`
❌ Don't use containers wider than 1280px
❌ Don't write "we" / "us" in customer-facing copy unless you're describing the company specifically (e.g., "MBA Rock is built by…")
