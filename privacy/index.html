Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

MBA Rock is operated by Better Together Branding ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. We try to keep it short and plain-spoken.

Short version: We collect the minimum needed to deliver the program — your name, email, payment confirmation from Stripe, and the lesson/Capstone progress you create. We do not sell your data. You can request a copy or deletion at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to mbarock.com, its subdomains, and the CDN at joshwark.github.io/mbarock-cdn where the course pages are served. It applies to people who visit the site, purchase the program, or interact with the support email.

2. Information we collect

You give us directly

  • Account info: name and email address you provide at signup or checkout.
  • Payment info: processed by Stripe. We receive a confirmation token and the last four digits of your card. We never store full card numbers.
  • Course work: the contents of your Capstone Business Plan fields, lesson notes, and quiz answers — saved to our database so they persist across devices.

Collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, lessons completed, quiz attempts, time spent. We use this to improve the program.
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, screen size, IP address (truncated for analytics).
  • Cookies and local storage: a member-id is stored in your browser's localStorage so we can recognize you between visits.

3. How we use it

  • Deliver the course program you purchased and persist your progress.
  • Issue certificates verifying your completion.
  • Send transactional emails (purchase confirmations, password resets).
  • Improve the program (which lessons cause confusion, which quizzes need re-tuning).
  • Send occasional product updates — you can opt out at any time.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share your Capstone contents with anyone outside our team unless you explicitly choose to share (e.g., by sending your Dossier export).
  • We do not use your course work to train AI models for other customers.

5. Third parties we work with

We use a small number of well-known services to operate the program. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe — payment processing. stripe.com/privacy
  • Squarespace — website hosting and member authentication. squarespace.com/privacy
  • Supabase — database for your Capstone, notes, and progress. supabase.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages — content delivery network for course pages.
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymized usage analytics. policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Opt out of marketing emails (transactional emails will still send while your account is active).
  • Withdraw consent for analytics by using a browser-level "Do Not Track" signal or clearing your cookies.

Email better.together.branding@gmail.com with any of the above and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We retain your account and course data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your Capstone contents, notes, and progress within 30 days. We retain anonymized purchase records for tax/legal compliance (typically 7 years).

8. International users

If you visit from the European Union, United Kingdom, or another region with strong privacy law, you have the same rights as listed above plus any additional rights granted by your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR right of portability). Email us to exercise those rights.

9. Children

MBA Rock is built for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we will email active members and update the "last updated" date at the top.

11. Contact

Questions, requests, complaints: better.together.branding@gmail.com
